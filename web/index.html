Ontario Building Code 3.2.3 limits the unprotected openings (windows, doors without the required rating) in a wall that faces a property line, so fire is less likely to jump between buildings. The permitted percentage comes from Table 3.2.3.1.B for a building that is not sprinklered throughout (Groups A, C, D and F Division 3) and depends on the limiting distance, the area of the exposing building face, and its length-to-height ratio. This quick tool covers the verified rows for faces of 10, 15 and 20 m² — the full table continues to much larger faces, and sprinklered buildings use Tables 3.2.3.1.D and 3.2.3.1.E.
